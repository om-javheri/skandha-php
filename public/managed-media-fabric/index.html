HERO products

Mediaexcel's HERO line covers live, file-based and event encoding, synchronisation and management, proven by Pay-TV operators and video platforms for two decades.

HERO Live Encoder

Multi-device, multi-channel adaptive bitrate encoding in HEVC, AVC and VVC, on-premise, virtual or in the cloud.

HERO File Encoder

Watch-folder VoD transcoding in parallel batches, linked to your CMS and MAM, with automatic quality checks.

HERO Event Encoder

Rugged, low-latency remote encoding at the venue, with failover and remote control.

HERO Synchronizer

Timecode carried in a sub-audio track so camera feeds line up frame by frame.

Remote Management System

One dashboard for every HERO unit across sites, with alerts, roles and automatic recovery.
